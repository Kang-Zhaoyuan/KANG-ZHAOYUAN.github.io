## Overview
Flipo Flip explores the motion and energy dissipation of an asymmetric rolling object. The work combines physical experiments, video tracking, geometric analysis, dynamical simulation, and comparisons with theoretical models.

## Methods
- Parametric 3D geometry and rolling-contact simulation.
- Frame-by-frame tracking and rolling-cycle segmentation.
- Support-point geometry, gravitational potential, and energy-loss analysis.
- Comparison of theoretical trajectories with numerical simulation and experiment.
