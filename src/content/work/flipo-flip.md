## Overview / 项目简介
This project investigates asymmetric rolling and energy dissipation through experiments, numerical models, geometry analysis, and cross-validation.

项目结合视频实验、逐帧追踪、几何建模、数值仿真与能量损耗分析，研究非对称物体的翻滚动力学。

## Methods / 方法
- 3D geometry and dynamic simulation / 三维几何与动力学仿真
- Video tracking, cycle segmentation, and experimental data cleaning / 视频追踪、周期切分和数据清洗
- Energy-barrier models and experimental cross-validation / 能量势垒建模与实验交叉验证

The animation above is a presentation-ready 3D simulation of a Group A configuration at an initial angular velocity of 15 rad/s, slowed to 0.4×.
