## Overview
A mathematical modeling project for Problem A of the 2026 China Undergraduate Mathematical Contest in Modeling, focused on coupled heat and mass transfer during hot-air drying of porous materials.

## Numerical methods
- Finite-volume discretization and Python solvers.
- Spatially resolved heat and moisture transport.
- Shrinkage geometry and conservation checks.
- Reproducible plots, computation results, and a complete LaTeX manuscript.

The illustration shows a finite-volume control region and heat fluxes across its boundaries.
