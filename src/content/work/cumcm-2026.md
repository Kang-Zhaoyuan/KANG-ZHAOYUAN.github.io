## Overview / 项目简介
A mathematical modeling project for Problem A of the 2026 China Undergraduate Mathematical Contest in Modeling, addressing heat and mass transfer during hot-air drying of porous materials.

2026 年全国大学生数学建模竞赛 A 题项目，研究中药材热风干燥过程中的热质耦合传输。

## Approach / 技术路线
- Finite-volume discretization and numerical solvers / 有限体积离散与数值求解
- Conservation diagnostics and evolving geometry / 守恒性验证与几何变化
- Automated scientific plots and a complete LaTeX paper / 自动化科学绘图与完整 LaTeX 论文
